About Sweet Crust

More than a bakery — a family kitchen, scaled with care.

Our Story

It started with one oven and a stack of handwritten recipes.

In 1998, our founder Meera opened a single-room bakery with a clay oven and recipes inherited from her mother. What began as a small sweets counter for the neighbourhood slowly grew into a full bakery — bread, pastries, chocolates and festive gifting, all made under one roof.

Three generations later, the ovens are bigger and the kitchen is certified for modern hygiene standards, but every recipe still traces back to that first notebook. Nothing about the taste was ever allowed to change.

Our Philosophy

Baking is a promise, not a process.

Three principles guide everything that leaves our kitchen.

Nothing Artificial

No preservatives, no artificial colours, no shortcuts in the mixing bowl.

Time Is An Ingredient

Slow ferments and long proofs, because rushing changes the flavour.

Made For Sharing

Every recipe is built with celebrations and family tables in mind.

Ingredients & Craftsmanship

Good ingredients don't need much help.

We work directly with local dairies for fresh cream and ghee, regional mills for stone-ground flour, and trusted growers for dry fruits and cocoa. Every ingredient is chosen for flavour first — not shelf life.
