Behind The Bakery

A single morning, from starter to shelf.

Every batch follows the same unhurried timeline — because good bread and honest sweets can't be rushed, no matter how busy the counter gets.

4:00 AM

Dough Meets Time

Sourdough starters and laminated doughs that rested overnight come out for shaping.

6:00 AM

First Bake

Ovens hit temperature and the first breads and croissants go in.

9:00 AM

Sweets Kitchen Opens

Mithai is finished fresh, tempered chocolate is poured and piped.

11:00 AM

Counter Ready

Everything is boxed, labelled and set out warm for the day.
