Our Story

A family recipe book, grown into a neighbourhood bakery.

Sweet Crust began in a small kitchen with one clay oven and a stack of handwritten recipes passed down from our founder's mother. Today those same recipes guide every loaf, sweet and chocolate we make — now paired with modern baking techniques, temperature-controlled proofing and stricter quality checks, without losing the taste that started it all.

  • Recipes unchanged for three generations
  • Ingredients sourced from trusted local farms
  • Traditional methods, modern hygiene standards
